About

I am an ophthalmology researcher and a faculty member in the Department of Optometry, School of Medicine and Health, at Guangzhou Xinhua University. I received my professional master’s degree in clinical medicine (ophthalmology) from Southern Medical University in 2024. My research focuses on quantitative retinal and choroidal imaging, vascular phenotyping, and clinically meaningful imaging biomarkers.

My experience includes ultra-widefield SS-OCTA, image-quality assessment, segmentation review, and clinical data analysis. I also develop Python/OpenCV imaging workflows and built FundusAgeAI, a PyTorch/ResNet18 project for chronological-age prediction from fundus photographs, with patient-level evaluation, image-quality analysis, and a publicly accessible Streamlit demo. I am seeking PhD opportunities in ophthalmic imaging and AI and welcome conversations with groups working on related clinical and computational research. Please feel free to contact me.

PROJECT 01 · 2026–presentLive demo available

FundusAgeAI

Chronological-age prediction from fundus photographs

Developed an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 regressor using 6,968 ODIR-5K fundus photographs from 3,484 patients, with patient-level data splits and staged fine-tuning. Deployed the frozen model as an interactive Streamlit demo.

MODEL MAE8.03 years95% bootstrap CI: 7.54–8.53
FIXED 59-YEAR BASELINE MAE8.83 yearsSame internal test set

Internal held-out evaluation · 523 patients · 1,046 images

Live Demo ↗
  • Python
  • PyTorch / ResNet18
  • Streamlit
  • Image QC
Evaluation & deployment details +
  • Study design: Patient-level training, validation, and test separation; staged fine-tuning followed by frozen-model evaluation.
  • Image quality & bias: Assessed age-dependent prediction bias and image-quality effects, including manual QC of 200 validation photographs and within-patient analysis of 36 discordant-quality fellow-eye pairs.
  • Reproducible deployment: The online demo uses the frozen, SHA-256-verified ResNet18 model and original preprocessing.

Research demonstration for chronological-age estimation. Results are from internal testing; no external or clinical validation is claimed.
