About

I completed a professional master’s degree in clinical medicine (ophthalmology) at Southern Medical University in 2024 and currently teach optometry at Guangzhou Xinhua University. During my master’s training, I investigated retinal and choroidal vascular features in pachychoroid spectrum diseases using ultra-widefield OCT/OCTA, contributing to first-author publications in ophthalmic imaging.

I am now building on this clinical imaging background through practical computational projects, including FundusAgeAI, a PyTorch-based model for estimating chronological age from fundus photographs. I am particularly interested in how quantitative retinal imaging and carefully evaluated computational methods can address clinically relevant questions. PROJECT 01 · 2026–presentLive demo available

FundusAgeAI

Chronological-age prediction from fundus photographs

Developed an ImageNet-pretrained ResNet18 regressor using 6,968 ODIR-5K fundus photographs from 3,484 patients, with patient-level data splits and staged fine-tuning. Deployed the frozen model as an interactive Streamlit demo.

MODEL MAE8.03 years95% bootstrap CI: 7.54–8.53
FIXED 59-YEAR BASELINE MAE8.83 yearsSame internal test set

Internal held-out evaluation · 523 patients · 1,046 images

Live Demo ↗
  • Python
  • PyTorch / ResNet18
  • Streamlit
  • Image QC
Evaluation & deployment details +
  • Study design: Patient-level training, validation, and test separation; staged fine-tuning followed by frozen-model evaluation.
  • Image quality & bias: Assessed age-dependent prediction bias and image-quality effects, including manual QC of 200 validation photographs and within-patient analysis of 36 discordant-quality fellow-eye pairs.
  • Reproducible deployment: The online demo uses the frozen, SHA-256-verified ResNet18 model and original preprocessing.

Research demonstration for chronological-age estimation. Results are from internal testing; no external or clinical validation is claimed.
