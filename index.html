About

I am an ophthalmology researcher and a faculty member in the Department of Optometry, School of Medicine and Health, at Guangzhou Xinhua University. I received my professional master’s degree in clinical medicine (ophthalmology) from Southern Medical University in 2024. My research focuses on quantitative retinal and choroidal imaging, vascular phenotyping, and clinically meaningful imaging biomarkers.

My experience includes ultra-widefield SS-OCTA, image-quality assessment, segmentation review, and clinical data analysis. I am extending this foundation through Python/OpenCV image processing and a PyTorch/ResNet18 fundus-age prediction project. I am seeking PhD opportunities in ophthalmic imaging and AI and welcome conversations with groups working on related clinical and computational research. Please feel free to contact me.

Research Interests

  • AI-assisted analysis of multimodal retinal imaging
  • Quantitative retinal and choroidal imaging biomarkers
  • Retinal and macular disease phenotyping
  • Image-based prediction of disease progression

PROJECT 01Ongoing · 2026–present

FundusAgeAI

Age prediction from fundus photographs

Implemented an initial PyTorch/ResNet18 baseline using patient-level training, validation, and test splits. Current work includes age-stratified, blinded image-quality review and exploring its relationship with prediction performance.

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • ResNet18
  • Image QC
Project approach +
  1. Organise fundus images and patient metadata.
  2. Separate training, validation, and test data at the patient level.
  3. Implement an age-prediction baseline.
  4. Review image quality and explore effects on performance.

An exploratory research and learning project; no clinical-use claim.
